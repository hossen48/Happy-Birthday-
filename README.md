# A little world for Adu ♡

A romantic click-based birthday website for Adu.

## Navigation
- Major pages use the **Before / Next** buttons at the bottom.
- The Love Archive is the only part with swipe interaction: swipe the photo card to change the photo and its matching reason together.
- Compatibility is one question per screen/card, with Before/Next controls and 10 questions.

## Add your photos
The ZIP intentionally does **not** include your photos.

Put your 30 files here:
`assets/photos/01.webp` through `assets/photos/30.webp`

Photos 01–10 are used in Love Archive. Photos 11–30 are used in Our Little Museum.

## Music
The included `assets/bgm/birthday-bgm.mp3` is the combined background track prepared for this site.

## Password
Visible hint: **The name I always call you**

Accepted answers in the script include: beautiful, pretty, love, cutie, Adu, and Pakhi.

Open `index.html` in a browser or publish the folder with GitHub Pages.
